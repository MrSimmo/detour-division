Levels are tuned by one lever, `recipe.hp`, and several only just pass, so re-run `npm run validate-levels` after any change to the sim, balance or bot, and re-tune whatever fails.

- `node tools/tune-level.ts data/levels/zN/zN-0M.json` searches `recipe.hp`. Standard levels stop once Heroic costs real lives. Sudden Death and Time Trial find the hardest scale the bot wins and back off 10% (ADR-0008).
- Changing the wave budget (`b0`) barely changes difficulty: bigger waves pay more bounty.
- **Fragile wins.** In the 2026-09-29 report, the one Standard level with 2 lives or fewer left on some difficulty is Three Craters, Heroic 2/20. The M3 list (Railyard Rumble, Bluster's Last Stand, Custard Oasis, Grinder's Glacier) no longer holds after the v1.1 to v1.4 re-tunes. Check `reports/levels.json` after each run.
- **Multi-route openings.** When a level loses early, look at `node tools/dev/leaks.ts <file> casual <strategy>` before lowering HP. Check a tunnel isn't a shortcut that bypasses the maze (The Last Roundabout had one), and that the opening cash covers the routes.
- **Time Trial targets** need enough spawns before the clock runs out. Count them before tuning. Ashfall Terrace needed waves every 8 s to be winnable at all.
- **Puzzles** are tuned by their purse. The plan in `bot.plan` is the reference solution (`generic: false`). `tools/dev/record-plan.ts` turns a winning bot run into a plan.
- **Puzzle plans break on any timing change.** Moving ground spawns 2 cells up the gate lanes (R-LANE-1, v1.3) broke 3 of the 4 reference plans, although every enemy was merely later; even 1 cell broke them. Arrival times shift against each tower's fire cycle, and the plans had no slack. `tools/dev/plan-search.ts` finds single tower moves that win again (Sludge Sluice needed two of its best singles combined). Pick by the 5-seed count, not the level seed alone: under the new timing the old plans won only 6–9 of 15. It happened again at v1.4 (ADR-0014: spawns 3.75 cells further up the lane). Cracked Ice needed one move (15/15). Sludge Sluice's best single move won only 10/15, and a pair of moves won 13/15; the pair search (the top 14 singles, every pair of them, over 5 seeds) was a scratch extension of `plan-search.ts`, about 60 lines. The same change also put three Standard levels' Heroic out of reach. The leaks were spread over many waves, with no stuck unit. A likely cause, not verified: slow units now arrive later than fast ones by more than before, so waves overlap more. `tune-level.ts` cut their HP scale by 20%.
- The Python scripts that first laid out the levels lived in a session scratchpad and are gone. Edit the JSON directly.
- **A long bot run means a stuck unit, not a slow level.** Check the longest `bot.seconds` in `reports/levels.json` after every validation run. At the v1.1 review, Grinder's Glacier Casual took 72 minutes of game time. A Glacier Grinder on its widest lane offset had wedged against a wall corner (R-MOVE-4). The replay-and-dump approach found it: rebuild the bot's final maze, replay the waves, and print what is still alive after the last launch.
