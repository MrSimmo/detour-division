How much of a gate lane is on screen depends on the window's shape, which the simulation can't know. So measure it over every level with `computeLayout`, and hide the far end of each lane under drawn trees (R-LANE-2, ADR-0014).

- **The see-through bands.** The HUD and the build bar have no background, and the canvas runs to the window's edges. So a lane shows through the letterbox beside the map, through the build-bar column (about 2 extra cells on the exit side), and on phones between the HUD plates. At v1.3, "2.75 cells is off screen on most displays" was checked in one screenshot and was wrong: desktop windows show up to 4.7 cells of an entrance lane and 6.9 of an exit lane. `tests/render/lanes.test.ts` now checks every level at four window sizes.
- **Up-and-down lanes show much less on desktop** (at most 2.7 cells, under the HUD band), so a bug on lanes that run across the screen can look fine on the others.
- **Drawn lanes are a whole number of 2-cell hedge pieces long.** A west or north lane's pieces are laid from its far end inwards, so an odd length pushed the last piece a cell into the field, with a straight cut. That showed only in a before-and-after crop of the gate.
- **Driving it in a browser:** `__spawn(type)` adds a unit at its spawn point. Setting `__game.speed = 0` freezes the simulation, and the leakers with it, without the pause menu's dimming. `__game.scene.leavers` lists the leakers still walking off. A boss spawned into a level that doesn't load its atlas group is drawn with no texture.
